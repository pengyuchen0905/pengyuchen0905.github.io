Kaleidoscopic Parametric Installation.

A built experiment in geometry, fabrication, and kaleidoscopic light.

Date
July 2023
Context
Design & Construction / Academic project
Group project
Methods
Grasshopper, Kangaroo, form-finding, panelisation, physical fabrication

My contribution

Concept development; Kangaroo-based form-finding; panel preparation; physical assembly.

From a relaxed surface to a built experience

Inspired by the optical principles of a kaleidoscope, the installation explores the relationship between computational form-finding and physical fabrication. A Kangaroo-relaxed surface is discretised into a panel system that can be prepared and assembled through interlocking components.

The fabrication process develops from a 1:10 exploratory model to a 1:5 structural prototype and a full-scale installation. Lightweight hollow panels lined with reflective laser films create shifting reflections and visual depth as visitors look through the openings or move within the structure.
