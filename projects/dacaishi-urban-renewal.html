Dacaishi Urban Renewal.

Urban renewal of the commercial area north of Dalian Railway Station.

Date
April – June 2024
Context
Dalian University of Technology / Academic project
Group project
Methods
Adaptive reuse, public-space design, commercial programming

My contribution

Design, modelling and drawings for the south-side building within the group proposal.

Location

Shuangxing Commodity Mall, Dalian, China

Instructor

Wu Liang

Reworking a district through public space

The proposal renews the Dacaishi commercial area while retaining its cultural memory. The framework of Shuangxing Commodity City is adapted, and the surrounding urban fabric is reorganised through an interconnected system of streets, passages and public spaces.

Five thematic experiences guide the commercial programme: relaxation, nature, immersion, social interaction, and identity expression. Flexible layouts, large steps and connecting corridors support different patterns of occupation throughout the day and evening.
