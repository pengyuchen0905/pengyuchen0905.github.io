New Acropolis Museum.

The continuity of history and the memory of space.

Individual project
Methods

My contribution

Location

Instructor

Chen Ting

Reading history through an architectural sequence

Set within the Acropolis of Athens, the academic museum proposal explores how architecture can convey the changing histories of a place. Historical and axial studies inform a spatial sequence that moves between archaeological traces, galleries, and the surrounding landscape.

The design uses layout, light and materials to create distinct emotional conditions. The galleries become a means of experiencing continuity and change, placing the encounter with artefacts within a broader narrative of the site’s cultural memory.
